# Interview rubric

Collect evidence in this order while keeping the conversation natural.

| Area | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Problem | Specific recurring event, frequency, volume, cost of inaction |
| Present | Current workaround, owner, participants, systems, observed bottleneck |
| Reality | One recent example and, when possible, direct observation or raw artifact |
| Outcome | Measurable target, current baseline, horizon, business success |
| Decisions | State inspected, evidence used, judgment made, bounded actions available |
| Feedback | Signal, source, delay, attribution quality, learning opportunity |
| Controls | Approval points, forbidden actions, budget, stop, escalation, rollback |
| Fit | Narrowest wedge, alternatives, reversibility, leverage, future fit |

Do not accept “save time,” “improve SEO,” or “automate sales” without a baseline, owner, and observable outcome. If evidence is unavailable, record it as a blocker rather than inventing precision.
